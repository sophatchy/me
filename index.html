About Me

I am a Digital Content Creator and advocate for internet safety originally from Banteay Meanchey. With over a decade of experience in Cambodia's digital space, I am dedicated to empowering individuals through digital literacy and creative storytelling.

My mission is to promote Digital Safety Awareness by creating educational content that helps the community stay safe online. Beyond technology, I am passionate about exploring Cambodia's landscapes 📸 and sharing my travel journeys to inspire others.
